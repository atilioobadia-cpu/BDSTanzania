How to Join BDS Tanzania

Home How to Join

A Clear Membership Process for Practitioners, Institutions and Professional Partners.

Joining BDS Tanzania is a straightforward process designed to welcome professionals and institutions that are committed to quality business development services in Tanzania. The Society provides a structured entry path so that applicants understand what is required, how membership is reviewed and what happens after approval.

The joining process is open throughout the year and applies to eligible individual practitioners, emerging professionals and institutions whose work aligns with the objectives of BDS Tanzania. Each application is reviewed to confirm eligibility, determine the correct membership category and ensure alignment with the standards and mission of the Society.

Once admitted, members are expected to uphold the Constitution, follow the Code of Ethics and remain in good standing through payment of applicable fees and active professional conduct. In return, members gain access to the national BDS Tanzania platform, learning opportunities, updates and professional community benefits.

  • Submit a membership application through the website or Secretariat.
  • Applications are reviewed for eligibility and correct membership category.
  • Admission decisions are communicated formally in writing.
  • Approved applicants complete fee payment to activate membership.
  • Open to individual professionals, associates and institutional applicants.
  • Members join a standards-driven national professional body.
  • Membership can begin at any time during the year.

Steps to Join BDS Tanzania

The membership journey follows a practical process from application to activation. This helps applicants understand expectations clearly and ensures that all new members enter the Society through a consistent and transparent procedure.

01.
Complete and Submit Your
Application

Start by submitting your membership application through the website or by contacting the Secretariat directly. Individual applicants should provide their professional profile, relevant qualifications, BDS-related experience and contact details. Institutional applicants should provide an organization profile and indicate how their work aligns with BDS Tanzania objectives.

02.
Eligibility Review and Membership
Category Approval

The Executive Committee reviews submitted applications to confirm eligibility and determine the appropriate category such as Full Professional Member, Associate Member or Institutional Member. This step ensures that applicants are placed within the right membership pathway based on their profile and professional standing.

03.
Pay Fees and Activate Your
Membership

Once approved, applicants are guided on the applicable entrance fee and annual subscription fee. Membership becomes active upon confirmation of payment. After activation, members begin enjoying access to Society updates, programmes, professional opportunities and participation rights according to their membership category.

About BDS Tanzania

BDS Tanzania (the Business Development Society of Tanzania) is the national professional association uniting business development service providers, enterprise advisors and institutional partners to promote standards, capacity building, advocacy and practical enterprise support for inclusive economic growth in Tanzania.
