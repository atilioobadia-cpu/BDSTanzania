Leadership and Governance Structure

BDS Tanzania is guided by a leadership structure that provides strategic direction, institutional oversight, programme coordination and stakeholder engagement in support of enterprise growth and professional business development services across Tanzania.

Dr. Alphonce Dotto Massaga

President, BDS Tanzania

The President provides overall leadership to the society, supports institutional direction, represents BDS Tanzania in strategic engagements, and promotes the growth of a strong and credible business development services ecosystem.

Governance Leadership

Strategic Direction and Institutional Oversight

The governance structure of BDS Tanzania supports policy guidance, accountability, leadership coordination, and institutional development to ensure the society remains aligned with its mission, standards and growth priorities.

This leadership function is central to strengthening partnerships, promoting professional conduct, and ensuring that programmes and member services are delivered in a coordinated and credible manner.

It also helps the society to maintain sound decision-making processes, support implementation of strategic priorities, encourage transparency, and uphold the values that guide BDS Tanzania in serving members, partners and the broader business community.

Through effective governance, BDS Tanzania is better positioned to respond to emerging enterprise needs, promote collaboration across sectors, and sustain a strong institutional foundation for long-term impact in Tanzania.

About BDS Tanzania

BDS Tanzania (the Business Development Society of Tanzania) is the national professional association uniting business development service providers, enterprise advisors and institutional partners to promote standards, capacity building, advocacy and practical enterprise support for inclusive economic growth in Tanzania.
