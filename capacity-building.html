Capacity Building

Home Capacity Building

Strengthening the Knowledge, Skills and Professional Competence of BDS Practitioners Across Tanzania.

The quality of enterprise support in Tanzania is directly determined by the quality of the practitioners who deliver it. BDS Tanzania places capacity building at the centre of its institutional mandate, investing in the professional development of business development service providers, trainers, enterprise facilitators and consultants so that they can deliver higher-quality, more consistent and more impactful services to the enterprises, cooperatives and entrepreneurs they serve.

Capacity building through BDS Tanzania covers the full spectrum of professional development — from foundational business advisory skills and technical training to advanced facilitation methodologies, enterprise coaching techniques, financial literacy advisory, sector-specific knowledge and professional ethics. We deliver capacity building through structured training programs, workshops, seminars, conference events, mentoring, peer learning networks and continuous professional development frameworks that keep members at the frontier of the profession.

We also build institutional capacity — supporting organizations, development programs, NGOs and government agencies to strengthen their internal business development service delivery systems, staff competence, program quality and professional standards. BDS Tanzania is the nationally coordinating body for practitioner capacity development in Tanzania's BDS ecosystem.

  • Structured professional training programs for business development service providers and enterprise advisors.
  • Continuous professional development (CPD) frameworks and learning pathways for BDS practitioners.
  • Workshops, seminars and short courses on enterprise advisory, facilitation, coaching and sector-specific BDS.
  • Annual conferences, symposia and knowledge-sharing events for the BDS practitioner community.
  • Mentoring program connecting experienced practitioners with emerging business development professionals.
  • Institutional capacity building for organizations delivering enterprise support program across Tanzania.
  • Online and blended learning resources supporting flexible professional development for practitioners nationally.

Key Focus Areas in Capacity Building

BDS Tanzania strengthens practitioners and institutions through structured learning and development pathways that improve the quality of enterprise support delivery. The focus is on professional growth, facilitation capability and institutional performance improvement.

01.
Continuous Professional Development (CPD)
for BDS Practitioners

BDS Tanzania develops and promotes a structured CPD framework for business development service providers — covering annual learning requirements, accredited training program, recognized learning activities and professional development records that support practitioner growth and professional standing.

02.
Training of Trainers and Enterprise
Facilitation Skills

We strengthen the facilitation and training competencies of BDS practitioners through dedicated training-of-trainers program, adult learning methodologies, enterprise facilitation techniques and practical workshop design skills that improve the quality of learning experiences delivered to enterprises.

03.
Institutional Capacity Building for
Enterprise Support Organizations

We work with NGOs, government institutions, development programs and private firms to strengthen their internal BDS delivery systems, staff professional competence, program quality standards and institutional performance — improving the effectiveness of organized enterprise support at all levels.

Frequently asked questions

BDS Tanzania supports capacity building across multiple dimensions: practitioner training and professional development, continuous professional development (CPD) frameworks, workshops and seminars, mentoring programs, peer learning networks, institutional capacity building and annual knowledge events for the BDS community.

All BDS Tanzania members have access to capacity building programs as a core membership benefit. Development partners, institutions, government agencies and enterprise support organizations can also engage BDS Tanzania for institutional capacity building, training program design and facilitation services.

Continuous professional development (CPD) is central to BDS Tanzania's vision of a competent, credible and continuously improving practitioner community. The Society promotes structured CPD requirements, recognized learning pathways and professional development records that help members stay current, improve their skills and demonstrate ongoing professional commitment.

Better-trained practitioners deliver better enterprise support. When BDS providers have stronger facilitation skills, deeper technical knowledge, clearer ethical frameworks and access to current tools and methodologies, the enterprises they serve receive higher-quality advisory support — with better business outcomes, stronger formalization rates and improved MSME competitiveness.

About BDS Tanzania
