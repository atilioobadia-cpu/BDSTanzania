About BDS Tanzania

BDS Tanzania (the Business Development Society of Tanzania) is the national professional association uniting business development service providers, enterprise advisors and institutional partners to promote standards, capacity building, advocacy and practical enterprise support for inclusive economic growth in Tanzania.
