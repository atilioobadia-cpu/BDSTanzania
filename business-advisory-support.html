Business Advisory Support

Home Business Advisory Support

Practical Business Advisory Services That Help Enterprises Grow, Formalize and Compete.

Business advisory support is the backbone of enterprise development. For Tanzania's millions of MSMEs, cooperatives, entrepreneurs and economic empowerment groups, access to credible, affordable and practical business advisory services can mean the difference between enterprise survival and sustainable growth. BDS Tanzania facilitates and promotes professional business advisory support across the country — delivered by trained, standards-driven practitioners who understand the real challenges of Tanzania's enterprise landscape.

Through our national network of business development service providers, we connect enterprises to advisory expertise in business planning, regulatory compliance, business formalization, financial readiness, productivity improvement, governance, digitalization and market development. Our practitioners are not generalists offering theoretical frameworks — they are field-level advisors equipped to engage MSMEs where they are, understand their specific constraints and deliver practical, actionable support that builds business capability and drives measurable results.

BDS Tanzania's approach to business advisory support is anchored in professional standards, ethical practice and continuous quality improvement. We support our members in delivering advisory services that enterprises can trust, partners can confidently commission and development programmes can integrate into structured enterprise support programming.

  • Business planning, feasibility assessment and enterprise growth strategy development.
  • Business formalization, registration, regulatory compliance and business licensing support.
  • Financial readiness, bookkeeping, financial management and access-to-finance preparation.
  • Enterprise governance, organizational structure and business management systems.
  • Market development, customer acquisition, sales strategy and value chain positioning.
  • Productivity improvement, operational efficiency and business process optimization.
  • Digitalization readiness, ICT adoption and business management technology integration.

Key Sub-Areas of Business Advisory Support

BDS Tanzania promotes structured business advisory support that responds to the practical needs of enterprises at different stages of development. The focus is on helping businesses formalize, strengthen financial readiness, improve strategic direction and compete more effectively in the market.

01.
MSME Formalization and Regulatory
Compliance Advisory

We support enterprises through the formalization process — business registration, tax compliance, licensing, regulatory engagement and governance structure development — reducing informality and improving access to formal markets, finance and institutional support programmes.

02.
Financial Readiness and
Access-to-Finance Support

We prepare enterprises for financial engagement — developing bankable business plans, building financial management systems, improving financial literacy, strengthening credit readiness and connecting enterprises to appropriate finance mechanisms and investment opportunities.

03.
Enterprise Growth Strategy and
Market Development Advisory

We work with enterprises to develop realistic, evidence-based growth strategies — covering market analysis, customer development, competitive positioning, value chain integration, product and service improvement and strategic partnership development.

Frequently asked questions

Business advisory support covers the full range of practical guidance, problem-solving and strategic assistance that business development service providers deliver to enterprises. It matters because most MSMEs in Tanzania lack internal capacity for business planning, financial management, compliance and market strategy and quality advisory support bridges that gap, reducing failure rates and accelerating sustainable growth.

BDS Tanzania's members (consultants, business advisors, enterprise coaches, trainers and sector specialists) deliver business advisory services. The Society promotes professional standards, ethics and quality assurance to ensure that advisory support delivered through the BDS Tanzania network is credible, practical and impactful.

Micro, small and medium enterprises across all sectors, cooperatives, youth and women-led businesses, economic empowerment groups, social enterprises and emerging entrepreneurs. The Society particularly prioritizes enterprises that are transitioning from informality to formal operations, accessing finance for the first time or developing strategies for market growth.

Enterprises can connect through the Contact Us page, email info@bdst.or.tz or call 0655-211777. BDS Tanzania can match enterprises with qualified business advisors, refer them to appropriate program support or facilitate connections to relevant partners and service providers.
